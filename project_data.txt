# Online beer shopping
BeerBox is an online marketplace for discovering and ordering unique beers with a simple, friendly shopping flow.

Timeline
2025 Feb
Industry
E-commerce

## Challenge
Customers had trouble finding unique beers locally, trusting freshness online, and placing larger orders for events.

## Approach
- Researched convenience shoppers, rare beer fans, and event planners.
- Focused the flow on product discovery, freshness signals, bulk ordering, and clear checkout.
- Built wireframes and prototypes, then refined labels, delivery options, and visual hierarchy through feedback.

## Result
The final concept creates a relaxed e-commerce experience that makes beer discovery, bulk ordering, and doorstep delivery feel clear and easy.

# VVS
VVS is a redesign concept for Stuttgart's regional public transport app, focused on clearer route planning and more reliable trip continuity.

Timeline
2024 Dec
Industry
Transportation App

## Challenge
The existing app had useful real-time transit data, but users struggled with unclear navigation, lost routes, weak hierarchy, and limited map interaction.

## Approach
- Reviewed app-store feedback and public ratings to identify recurring pain points.
- Prioritized platform visibility, saved routes, clearer departure and arrival states, and route continuity.
- Reworked key screens through wireframes, prototypes, and user testing.

## Result
The redesign creates a cleaner transit experience with easier route planning, clearer station information, and a stronger foundation for future ticket and account features.

## Next Steps
- Simplify ticket selection.
- Add account management.
- Support buying tickets for another person.
- Add tappable station and route information on the map.

# MealMate
Your personal online nutrition assistant
My Role
Designer responsible for UX research, user flows, wireframes, and high-fidelity UI design.
Team:
Guided by two Senior UI/UX designer peers.

## Challenge
- Finding recipes that match their dietary preferences and restrictions.
- Understanding and maintaining a balanced diet.
- Staying motivated to consistently track and improve nutrition habits.

## Solutions
- Custom Recipe Matching - Filter by diet, ingredients, and time.
- Personalized Meal Suggestions - AI recommends meals based on diet and preferences.
- Flexible Progress Tracking - Simple feedback and visual goals keep users motivated.

## Process
- Research & Analysis: Examined market, competitors, and user pain points to shape core app features.
- User Interviews: Identified the need for flexible, intuitive meal tracking and planning.
- Hypothesis: Quick, adaptive, AI-driven meal suggestions can improve user engagement and consistency.
- User Persona: Targeted busy professionals needing simple meal planning and progress tracking.
- Wireframing & Prototyping: Developed and refined user flows through low- to mid-fidelity wireframes and testing.
- Visual Design & Style Guide: Used clean typography, bright accents, and clear icons for readability and vibrancy.
- Next Steps:Expand task flows based on user feedback.Further usability testing for design refinements.
Next Steps:

## Conclusion
Mealmate streamlines meal tracking and promotes healthier habits. Our design, shaped by user interviews and competitor insights, focuses on intuitive navigation, quick logging, and engaging visual feedback. Early tests confirmed a clean layout and effective progress cues.
