## My role
This project highlights visual and spatial design work for Smina during my time at UP Design Studio, included:
- Interior design concepts for new shop locations
- Virtual staging to support the redesign of existing stores
- 360 virtual walking tours
- High-quality product renders used in advertising and internal presentations
