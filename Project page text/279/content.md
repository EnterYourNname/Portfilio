# CACCAROdesign concept

## Disclaimer
Designed a furniture concept for the Italian furniture brand Caccaro. The submission won first place and was selected by the company’s representatives
Industry
Industry
Interior Design & Furniture

## My role
As one of two designers on the project, I developed the concept and produced all 3D visualizations for Caccaro, using 3ds Max and Photoshop. Beyond the renders, I led the initial research, created mood boards, and selected materials to ensure every detail aligned with Caccaro’s brand aesthetic. The final interior concept provided the factory with fresh stylistic ideas, earning first place and direct approval from the company’s representatives.
